EXAM WRITING ▣ Install on exam devices

02 / SECURE EXAM WRITING

EduExamWriter

A lightweight exam-writing app for student devices, with kiosk-style exam lockdown and the writing tools students need, including a character map for accented characters. Designed to support centres with JCQ exam requirements; schools should check the software and their setup against current guidance before use.

Kiosk / exam lockdown Autosave & backups Configurable spell check Word, PDF & printing Local, USB or network saving

Set up for your centre and exam

01

Deploy your way
Install manually, with Microsoft Intune or Group Policy. Configure options in an editable JSON settings file; add two optional start-screen questions if needed.

02

Start ready for candidates
Can launch at startup on devices using generic logins. Students enter their name, exam reference, candidate and centre numbers, plus any configured details.

03

Write, finish and save
Allow students to finish or require an invigilator to end the exam. Save as Word, PDF or both, print, and store locally, in home folders, on USB or to a network share.

04

Produce a clear exam document
Optional cover page, candidate and exam details in the header, and page X of X numbering in the footer.

A practical point of view

Built from the
school side of things.

I’m a computer technician who has spent the last 10 years working in education. I’ve seen how small, recurring tasks can take time away from the people and work that matter most.

EduTools is where I share free software ideas shaped by that experience. I bring the problems, context, and prompts; AI generates the software. The aim is practical: make useful tools for teachers and the staff who support them.
